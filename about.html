About this project

Supporting better crane motor maintenance

A student project concept for monitoring motor condition and helping maintenance teams plan their work.

Project goal

The concept explores how temperature, electrical current, and vibration readings could help engineers notice changes in crane motor condition and plan inspections before faults lead to avoidable downtime.

A future system could bring together crane-level monitoring, maintenance records, alerts, and planned work in one place for authorized personnel.

How this demo should be read

This front-end is an early interface prototype. The crane IDs, sensor readings, warnings, dates, and graphs are illustrative sample content. They do not come from KPA equipment and are not live measurements.

Prototype status: This page is not an official KPA service, is not connected to crane control systems, and does not provide operational or safety advice.

Possible next steps

Further development would require validating the concept with relevant engineers, agreeing sensor locations and safe data collection methods, defining maintenance thresholds, and designing secure access to real equipment data.
