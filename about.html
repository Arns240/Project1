About this project

Monitoring machine condition

A student project concept for using equipment data to support maintenance planning across different industries.

Project goal

The concept explores how temperature, electrical current, and vibration readings could help maintenance teams notice changes in machine condition and plan inspections before faults lead to avoidable downtime.

The interface demonstrates an overview of six machines, sample condition alerts, and planned maintenance information. It is designed as a general concept that could be adapted to different industries.

How to read this demo

This is an early interface prototype. Machine names, sensor readings, warnings, dates, and graphs are illustrative sample content. They do not come from connected equipment and are not live measurements.

Prototype status: This page is not an operational monitoring service and does not provide machine safety advice.

Possible next steps

Further development would require selecting representative equipment, validating sensor placement and safe data collection, agreeing machine-specific thresholds, and designing secure access to real readings and maintenance records.
